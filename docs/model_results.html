kraken CTC + LM leads on character accuracy (0.9744) and CER; the per-position char-LM rescorer fixes minim substitutions on top of the CTC model.

Catmus, the frozen off-the-shelf CATMuS-medieval baseline, leads on word accuracy (0.8566) and WER (0.1434) — it makes the fewest whole-word errors, even though its character accuracy sits mid-field.

Medusa, a 9-billion-parameter vision-language model, is last on every metric — the 4-million-parameter kraken pipeline is both more accurate and ~2000× smaller.

Validation = 300 held-out line crops of the AlbucE manuscript. All figures on the diplomatic (base-letter) transcription. Catmus and Medusa are scored on the full-page eval (their fair protocol); the fine-tuned kraken and TrOCR models on the native line-crop eval.
